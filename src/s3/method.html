How this was built

  • I read all of Chapter 3 of the Edition 4 workbook (June 2025) and the Element 3 learning objectives in its syllabus map.
  • I went through the book's 80 practice questions and the Chapter 3 end-of-chapter questions, and recorded which Element 3 objectives each one tests. The priority labels in Learn come from that count.
  • Real CISI exam questions are confidential and are not published, so no one can give you "real" questions. The {{NQ}} here are original and follow the book's style: short extracts, four-company tables, and "which statement is correct".
  • Every calculation was worked through by hand. Each explanation names the workbook section so you can check it.
  • EPS, P/E, dividend yield and Gordon growth are taught in Chapter 3, but the syllabus counts them under Element 4. Only EPS appears here, because it is part of building the income statement.
