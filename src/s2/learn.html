Rd = (1 + Rf) × (1 + Rfx) − 1

Start GBP/USD rate ÷ End GBP/USD rate − 1
VWAP
Σ(Price × Volume) ÷ Σ Volume
Beta in practice
Expected move = β × Market move
β 1.5 = 50% more volatile · β 0.7 = 30% less

Traps the wrong answers rely on

  • Systematic ≠ systemic. Market-wide falls are systematic risk. A collapse of the financial system is systemic risk.
  • Beta 1.2 means 20% more volatile than the market, not 20% outperformance (MCQ 4).
  • CFL, not ATR, drives the final risk profile when the two conflict.
  • Suitability doesn't apply to execution-only. The ability to bear losses can only be assumed for per se professionals.
  • Churning is excessive trading for commission. Client churn is clients leaving the firm.
  • Best client communication, per the book: a real-time web portal (MCQ 71).
  • CCPs reduce counterparty risk. Moving OTC trades onto exchanges reduces it too (MCQ 70).
  • VWAP is best used intraday (MCQ 61).
  • HFT's commonly quoted downside is the illusion of liquidity (MCQ 41).
  • OTFs can't trade equities. AIM is an MTF.
  • Hedge currency on overseas bonds; it is often not done for diversified overseas equity portfolios.
  • Puts keep the upside; futures and forwards give it up.
