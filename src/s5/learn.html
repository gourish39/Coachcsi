Annual rent ÷ Property value
Net rental yield
(Annual rent − Operating costs) ÷ Property value

Traps the wrong answers rely on

  • Unit trust investors are beneficiaries of a trust, not trustees and not parties to a contract (MCQ 2).
  • Open-ended funds can't trade at a discount to NAV; closed-ended ones can. A trust being wound up usually has a small discount (MCQ 22). Discount is measured against NAV (MCQ 48).
  • OCF excludes performance fees; TER includes them. Neither includes entry or exit charges.
  • Rising market → offer basis. Falling market → bid basis.
  • ETN = unsecured bank debt (credit risk, no interest, no votes). ETC = fully collateralised, not UCITS.
  • Leveraged ETFs aim for a multiple of the daily return, not the long-term return.
  • Structured deposits have FSCS cover; structured investments don't. Capital protection always depends on the issuer.
  • Reverse tracker = rises when the index falls (MCQ 11).
  • Hedge funds are usually unauthorised or offshore, target absolute (not relative) returns, and have low correlation with markets (MCQ 33).
  • Property is heterogeneous (MCQ 44). The net yield uses annual figures: multiply monthly figures by 12 (MCQ 58).
  • Impact bonds fund prevention and early intervention (MCQ 21). Blue bonds fund ocean projects.
  • Non-reporting fund gains are taxed as income, so the CGT allowance can't be used.
  • Owning an NFT doesn't give you the copyright.
Workbook inconsistency to know about. Section 2.3.1 says an ETP with "less than 60%" in fixed income pays interest, but every other section (unit trusts, OEICs, offshore funds) says more than 60% means interest. Use "more than 60% fixed income → interest" in the exam.
