The few formulas in Section 1

CAPM
E(R) = Rf + β × (Rm − Rf)
(Rm − Rf) is the market risk premium
Market capitalisation
Shares in issue × Share price
GNP / GNI
GNP = GDP + Net property income from abroad GNI = GDP + Net foreign factor income
Beta in practice
Expected move = β × Market move
β 1.2 = 20% more volatile than the market

Traps the wrong answers rely on

  • Systematic vs systemic: systematic is market risk that can't be diversified away. Systemic is the collapse of the whole financial system. A sharp fall in emerging markets is systematic risk (MCQ 8).
  • Beta 1.2 means 20% more volatile than the market, not 20% outperformance.
  • Strong-form EMH fails because of insider dealing laws, not money laundering or data rules.
  • Anomaly switch = identical bonds except price or yield. Policy switch = acting on an unpriced rate view.
  • Riding the yield curve only works with an upward-sloping curve.
  • Immunisation matches duration, not maturity.
  • Indexation: the first decision is choosing the right index for the client (MCQ 25).
  • Stock lending: the lender is protected by collateral (MCQ 3). It loses the vote but gets a manufactured dividend.
  • ECB target is close to but below 2%. The BoE target is 2% CPI. The Fed has a dual mandate.
  • PMI 50 means no change, not growth.
  • Perfect competition gives only normal profits in the long run (MCQ 57).
  • Loss aversion means losses feel about twice as strong. Don't confuse it with regret aversion (avoiding admitting a mistake) or the sunk cost fallacy (sticking with something because of past spending).
