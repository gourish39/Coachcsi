How this was built

  • I read Chapter 1 of the Edition 4 workbook (June 2025) against the Element 1 learning objectives in its syllabus map.
  • I matched each objective against the book's 80 practice questions and the Chapter 1 end-of-chapter questions to set the priority labels.
  • Real CISI exam questions are confidential and not published. These {{NQ}} questions are original and follow the workbook's style. Each explanation names the workbook section.
  • Hedge funds, managed futures, money market and manager-of-managers funds are listed under Element 1, but the workbook teaches them in Chapter 5. They are covered briefly here and in full in the Section 5 module.
