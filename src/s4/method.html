How this was built

  • I read Chapter 4 of the Edition 4 workbook (June 2025) and the investor-ratio sections of Chapter 3 (3.6), which the syllabus counts under Element 4.
  • I matched each Element 4 learning objective against the book's 80 practice questions and the Chapter 4 end-of-chapter questions to set the priority labels.
  • Real CISI exam questions are confidential and not published. These 50 questions are original and follow the workbook's style. Every calculation was worked through by hand, and each explanation names the workbook section.
  • Market-activity topics such as exchanges, VWAP and FX risk are printed at the end of Chapter 4, but the syllabus counts them under Element 2, so they are in the Section 2 module.
